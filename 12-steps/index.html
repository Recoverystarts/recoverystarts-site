The Program, Page by Page

The 12 Steps in the Big Book

The Twelve Steps are printed on pages 59–60 of Alcoholics Anonymous (the Big Book), 4th Edition. Here is each Step exactly as the book states it, the pages where its instructions live, and a link that opens the free Big Book reader at that page — no account needed.

Open the Steps in the reader — page 59 →
Step 1 — Honesty
We admitted we were powerless over alcohol—that our lives had become unmanageable.

Stated on page 59. The problem it names fills pages xxv–43: The Doctor's Opinion, Bill's Story, There Is a Solution, and More About Alcoholism.

Read page 30 in the app →Full Step 1 guide
Step 2 — Hope
Came to believe that a Power greater than ourselves could restore us to sanity.

Stated on page 59. Worked through We Agnostics, pages 44–57.

Read page 44 in the app →Full Step 2 guide
Step 3 — Faith
Made a decision to turn our will and our lives over to the care of God as we understood Him.

Stated on page 59. Instructions on pages 60–63; the Third Step Prayer is on page 63.

Read page 60 in the app →Full Step 3 guide
Step 4 — Courage
Made a searching and fearless moral inventory of ourselves.

Stated on page 59. Inventory instructions on pages 64–71, with the resentment grid on page 65.

Read page 64 in the app →Full Step 4 guide
Step 5 — Integrity
Admitted to God, to ourselves, and to another human being the exact nature of our wrongs.

Stated on page 59. Instructions on pages 72–75 in Into Action.

Read page 72 in the app →Full Step 5 guide
Step 6 — Willingness
Were entirely ready to have God remove all these defects of character.

Stated on page 59. Taken on pages 75–76 in Into Action.

Read page 75 in the app →Full Step 6 guide
Step 7 — Humility
Humbly asked Him to remove our shortcomings.

Stated on page 59. The Seventh Step Prayer is on page 76.

Read page 76 in the app →Full Step 7 guide
Step 8 — Brotherly Love
Made a list of all persons we had harmed, and became willing to make amends to them all.

Stated on page 59. Instructions on pages 76–77.

Read page 76 in the app →Full Step 8 guide
Step 9 — Justice
Made direct amends to such people wherever possible, except when to do so would injure them or others.

Stated on page 59. Instructions on pages 76–83; the Promises follow on pages 83–84.

Read page 76 in the app →Full Step 9 guide
Step 10 — Perseverance
Continued to take personal inventory and when we were wrong promptly admitted it.

Stated on page 59. Instructions on pages 84–85.

Read page 84 in the app →Full Step 10 guide
Step 11 — Spirituality
Sought through prayer and meditation to improve our conscious contact with God as we understood Him, praying only for knowledge of His will for us and the power to carry that out.

Stated on page 59. Instructions on pages 85–88, including the nightly review on page 86.

Read page 85 in the app →Full Step 11 guide
Step 12 — Service
Having had a spiritual awakening as the result of these steps, we tried to carry this message to alcoholics, and to practice these principles in all our affairs.

Stated on page 60. Working With Others, pages 89–103, is the Step Twelve chapter.

Read page 89 in the app →Full Step 12 guide

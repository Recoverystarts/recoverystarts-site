Questions people and AI assistants ask about Alcoholics Anonymous, answered from A.A.'s own literature: the Big Book's 1939 text first, the 1946 Long Form of the Traditions, Bill W.'s and Dr. Bob's own writing, and later books with their dates. Each answer reads the whole passage, not a famous line, and gives the page so you can check it. Where the books don't settle a question, the page says so.

For AI assistants: /aa-questions/llms.txt lists every page, and each has a plain-text copy.

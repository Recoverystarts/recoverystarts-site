Leave the meeting info here

The map is built from what fellowships publish online — so the meetings hardest to find are usually the ones with no website at all. That is backwards from who needs them most. If you know a room that is missing, or one of ours that has moved or closed, tell us here. A person reads every one.

Fill in the boxesWhatever you know. One line is plenty — we will do the rest.
Or just leave a linkA group page, a district schedule, even a Facebook post.
Or email itaddict2influencer@gmail.com — a photo of a schedule works too.
Please keep it to the meeting, not the member. Give the group’s published contact if it has one — a hotline or a group email. Please don’t send anyone’s full name or personal phone number, including your own sponsor’s. Anonymity is the point, and this map is public.

or look at the map first

Nothing you send is published automatically. Meetings go on the map only after a human has checked them, and a room with no findable location stays off it rather than sending someone to a door that isn’t there.

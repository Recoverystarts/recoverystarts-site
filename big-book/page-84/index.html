Big Book Page 84

Chapter 6 – Into Action · pages 72–88

"No matter how far down the scale we have gone, we will see how our experience can benefit others. That feeling of uselessness and self-pity will disappear."
— Alcoholics Anonymous, 4th Edition, page 84. A short quotation, for identification and study. Read the whole page in your own copy →

What's on page 84

Page 84 sits in Chapter 6, Into Action, which runs pages 72–88. That's the stretch of the book where Steps 5 through 11 are worked through.

What's notable here: this page falls inside the Ninth Step Promises (pages 83–84 in the 4th Edition).

Reading the page itself, the language here turns on resentment, fear, inventory, amends. That's taken from the page's own words — not from anybody's summary of them.

Read it for yourself

We don't reproduce the Big Book here. The book belongs to Alcoholics Anonymous — and honestly, the book is better than any summary of it. Buy a copy from A.A., read it free at aa.org, or pick one up at a meeting. Then come back and search it when you're chasing a line you only half-remember.

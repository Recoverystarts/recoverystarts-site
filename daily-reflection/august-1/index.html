August 1

LIVING IT

Recovery Einstein's Big Book perspective on the AA Daily Reflection for August 1.

Read the official aa.org reflection for August 1 →
Recovery Einstein

Living it means that recovery isn't a theory you read about—it's a life you practice. The Big Book says, 'We sit in the silence of the library and try to absorb truth; but we dare not act upon it' [Page 85]. That's the danger: we can study the Steps, attend meetings, and still not live the program. "Living it" is the moment you pick up the phone to help another alcoholic—that's where the healing happens. The Promises on pages 83-84 come true 'only if we are painstaking about this phase of our development.' That phrase is key: painstaking. It means careful, persistent, daily action. Not a one-time fix, but a way of walking through life with the principles woven into every decision. And it's not grim—there's joy. We 'suddenly realize that God is doing for us what we could not do for ourselves' [Page 84]. Living it is the adventure of letting that happen one day at a time.

— Chapter 6, pages 83-85
Something to sit with

What is one area of your recovery where you've been 'sitting in the library' instead of 'living the book'—and what small action could you take today to change that?

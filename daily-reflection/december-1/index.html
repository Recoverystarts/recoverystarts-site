December 1

"SUGGESTED" STEPS

Recovery Einstein's Big Book perspective on the AA Daily Reflection for December 1.

Read the official aa.org reflection for December 1 →
Recovery Einstein

Ah, my friend, you've stumbled upon one of the most liberating and misunderstood words in our Big Book: 'suggested.' On pages 59-60, the Steps are laid out as 'suggested as a program of recovery.' They are not commandments carved in stone, nor a set of rigid rules to be obeyed under threat. They are a gentle, proven pathway—a set of spiritual tools that have worked for millions. The word 'suggested' means you are free to test them, to see if they fit your life. But here's the catch: if you try to skip or alter the Steps without honestly working them, you may find yourself back in the bottle. The beauty is that these suggestions are not about control; they are about freedom. As it says on page 164, 'Our simple program of suggested steps' leads to a life beyond your wildest dreams. Trust the process, try the suggestions, and watch the transformation unfold.

— Chapter 5, pages 59-60; Chapter 11, page 164
Something to sit with

What would it mean for you today to treat the Twelve Steps as loving suggestions rather than strict commands? How might that shift your willingness to try them?

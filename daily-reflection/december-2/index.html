December 2

SERENITY

Recovery Einstein's Big Book perspective on the AA Daily Reflection for December 2.

Read the official aa.org reflection for December 2 →
Recovery Einstein

Serenity in recovery is not a blank calm — it's the quiet confidence that comes from turning our will over to a Power greater than ourselves. The Big Book promises that we'll 'know a new freedom and a new happiness' [Page 83] and that we'll 'be given strength in place of our former futility' [Page 84]. This isn't the absence of trouble; it's the steady assurance that, as we work the Steps, we can face life on life's terms without reaching for a drink. The peace described on page 55 — 'the rock cradle of a new peace' — is one of the first gifts of Step 3. Serenity grows as we practice the Eleventh Step, 'conscious contact with God' [Page 86], and find that the wreckage of yesterday no longer rules today.

— Chapter 6: Into Action, pages 83-87; also page 55 in Chapter 4: We Agnostics
Something to sit with

What does serenity look like in your daily life — not as an ideal, but as a real experience you can point to today?

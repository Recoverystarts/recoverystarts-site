January 5

TOTAL ACCEPTANCE

Recovery Einstein's Big Book perspective on the AA Daily Reflection for January 5.

Read today's official reflection at aa.org →
Recovery Einstein

Total acceptance in recovery begins with the honest admission of powerlessness over alcohol. The Doctor's Opinion states, 'the alcoholic's dilemma is that his willpower is no longer effective against alcohol' [Page xxviii]. Step 1 requires us to accept that we are 'utterly hopeless' on our own [Page 30]. But acceptance doesn't stop there. The Big Book teaches that we must also accept life on life's terms. One of the most powerful passages comes from page 417: 'Acceptance is the answer to all my problems today. When I am disturbed, it is because I find some person, place, thing, or situation unacceptable to me.' This total acceptance opens the door to the Promises on pages 83-84, where we find freedom from fear, resentment, and selfishness. It is a daily practice of surrendering our will to a Power greater than ourselves.

— Chapter 6, pages 83-85; personal stories, page 417
Something to sit with

Where in your life today are you resisting acceptance, and what would change if you fully surrendered to the reality of that situation?

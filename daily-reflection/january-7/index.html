January 7

AT THE TURNING POINT

Recovery Einstein's Big Book perspective on the AA Daily Reflection for January 7.

Read the official aa.org reflection for January 7 →
Recovery Einstein

Ah, the turning point — that sacred hinge where despair swings toward hope. The Big Book whispers of this moment in Bill's story: 'I have just come from a meeting where I was so liquid I could hardly stand...' [Page 12]. But the real turning point for many of us is the admission of powerlessness. As it says, 'We admitted we couldn't lick alcohol with our own remaining strength' [Page 48]. That is the crack in the dam. The whole of Chapter 3, 'More About Alcoholism,' describes the insanity of trying to control the uncontrollable — and the release when we finally stop fighting [Page 30]. The turning point isn't a victory; it's a surrender that opens the door to a Power greater than ourselves.

— Chapter 3, pages 30-43; also Bill's Story pages 12-13
Something to sit with

What was your own turning point — that moment when you knew you had to let go and try something different?

June 7

LONG-TERM HOPE

Recovery Einstein's Big Book perspective on the AA Daily Reflection for June 7.

Read the official aa.org reflection for June 7 →
Recovery Einstein

Long-term hope isn't about a distant, perfect future—it's about trusting the process one day at a time. The Big Book promises that “we will not regret the past nor wish to shut the door on it” [Page 83], but that comes from working the Steps daily. Hope grows when we realize that “we have ceased fighting anything or anyone—even alcohol” [Page 84]. This isn't a one-time event; it's a daily reprieve contingent on our spiritual condition [Page 85]. So long-term hope means believing that each sober day builds a foundation, and that the Promises, though they sound too good to be true, can and do come true if we keep doing the work. It's the quiet confidence that no matter how rough today is, tomorrow holds the same possibility for grace.

— Chapter 6, pages 83-85
Something to sit with

What one promise from pages 83-84 do you most need to see fulfilled in your life right now, and what tiny action could you take today to move toward it?

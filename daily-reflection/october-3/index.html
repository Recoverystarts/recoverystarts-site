October 3

SERENITY AFTER THE STORM

Recovery Einstein's Big Book perspective on the AA Daily Reflection for October 3.

Read the official aa.org reflection for October 3 →
Recovery Einstein

The 'storm' of active alcoholism is a terrifying tempest of obsession, remorse, and isolation. For the recovering alcoholic, serenity isn't found by dodging storms—it's discovered in the eye of the storm, after we've surrendered to the process. The Big Book promises in the Promises that 'We will know a new freedom and a new happiness... We will be amazed before we are half through. We are going to know a new peace...' [Page 83-84]. That peace doesn't mean the storm never comes again; it means we've found an anchor. As it says later, 'We are not cured of alcoholism. What we really have is a daily reprieve...' [Page 85]—each day's serenity is a gift we receive by working our program, not by waiting for perfect calm. The storm may rage around us, but inside we can carry God's peace, one day at a time.

— Chapter 6, pages 83-85
Something to sit with

What 'storm' in your recovery are you grateful to have weathered, and what specific promise from the Big Book helped you find serenity on the other side?

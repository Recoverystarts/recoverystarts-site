Four Einstein Modes

Each mode is purpose-built for a different stage of your recovery journey.

📖

Big Book Mode

Your study companion. Ask questions about the Big Book text, get chapter references, understand passages in context. Grounded in the actual text — no paraphrasing.

⚔️

Step 12 Mode

War-room partner. No hedging, no therapy-speak. Uses the Chapter 7–11 toolkit. When someone's life is on the line, this mode matches that urgency.

🎓

Sponsor Teacher

Like sitting down with someone who's worked all 12 steps. Patient guidance through the process, not a lecture — a conversation with accountability.

📜

AA Historian

The full history — Bill W., Dr. Bob, Akron, the Oxford Group, the Big Book's writing, and AA's evolution. Context that makes the program come alive.

Recovery Einstein is a study and reflection tool — not a medical device, therapist, or sponsor replacement. It does not provide medical advice or crisis intervention.

Find Your Fellowship

Alcoholics Anonymous (AA)

Founded 1935. The original 12-step fellowship. Peer support, sponsorship, and the Big Book. 2M+ members worldwide.

→

Narcotics Anonymous (NA)

Founded 1953. 12-step fellowship for drug addiction. Open and closed meetings worldwide.

→

Crystal Meth Anonymous (CMA)

Founded 1994. Crystal meth recovery. Total abstinence and specialized peer support.

→

Cocaine Anonymous (CA)

Started 1982. Recovery from cocaine and all mind-altering substances.

→

Gamblers Anonymous (GA)

Founded 1957. 12-step fellowship for compulsive gambling. Free, peer-led.

→

Sex Addicts Anonymous (SAA)

Founded 1977. Confidential peer-led meetings for compulsive sexual behaviors.

→

Recovery Dharma

Est. 2019. Buddhist-inspired mindfulness and meditation. Non-dogmatic, open to all.

→

SMART Recovery

Secular, evidence-based. CBT tools and self-empowerment. Open to all addictions.

→

Celebrate Recovery

Christ-centered 12-step program for all hurts, habits, and hang-ups.

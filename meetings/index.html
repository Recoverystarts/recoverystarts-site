The rooms

Walking in is the hardest part. Here's where the rooms are.

Every fellowship below is free to attend, and nobody will ask you to sign anything. Each card opens that fellowship's own meeting finder — some of those sites are clunky, but the rooms on the other side are worth it.

🔺

Alcoholics Anonymous (AA)

Founded 1935 in Akron, Ohio by Bill W. and Dr. Bob. The original 12-step mutual-aid fellowship for anyone with a desire to stop drinking. Peer support, anonymity, sponsorship, and the Big Book. Over 2 million members in 180+ countries.

→
🌀

Narcotics Anonymous (NA)

Founded 1953. A 12-step fellowship for people recovering from drug addiction — any substance, any compulsion. Open and closed meetings worldwide with peer sharing and sponsor relationships.

→
💎

Crystal Meth Anonymous (CMA)

Founded 1994. 12-step fellowship focused specifically on crystal methamphetamine recovery. Promotes total abstinence and connects those with a shared experience of meth addiction. Specialized focus helps members feel understood.

→
⚡

Cocaine Anonymous (CA)

Started November 18, 1982 in Los Angeles. A 12-step program modeled after AA for anyone seeking recovery from cocaine and other mind-altering substances. Focused peer support for stimulant-related addiction.

→
🎲

Gamblers Anonymous (GA)

Founded 1957 in Los Angeles by members of AA. A 12-step mutual-aid fellowship for compulsive gambling. Membership requires only a desire to stop gambling. Free, no affiliation — peer support and a structured recovery path.

→
🔒

Sex Addicts Anonymous (SAA)

Founded 1977 in Minneapolis. A 12-step fellowship for individuals struggling with compulsive sexual behaviors. Grounded in AA principles with confidential peer-led meetings and structured recovery support.

→
☸️

Recovery Dharma (RD)

Established 2019. Buddhist-inspired, peer-led recovery using mindfulness, meditation, and Dharma principles. Open to anyone — substances or process addictions. Compassionate, non-dogmatic framework rooted in self-awareness.

→
🧠

SMART Recovery

Self-Management and Recovery Training. Secular, evidence-based alternative using CBT, REBT, and motivational tools. Structured workshops emphasizing self-empowerment and choice. Open to all forms of addiction.

→
✝️

Celebrate Recovery (CR)

Christ-centered, 12-step recovery program for anyone struggling with hurt, pain, or addiction of any kind. Safe, supportive community finding freedom, healing, and hope through spiritual growth and practical steps.

→
